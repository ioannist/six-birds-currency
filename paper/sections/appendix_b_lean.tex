\section{Lean formalization}\label{app:lean}

The formal development lives under \path{lean/} and is written in Lean~4 with mathlib \cite{demoura2015lean,mathlib2020}. It uses toolchain \texttt{v4.28.0} and the mathlib revision pinned in \path{lean/lake-manifest.json} (\texttt{8f9d9cff}). It builds with \texttt{lake build}. The file \path{lean/CheckAxioms.lean} prints the transitive axioms of every principal theorem, and all of them depend only on \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, and \texttt{Quot.sound}. The development contains no \texttt{sorry}, \texttt{admit}, or added axioms.

\subsection{Finite KL and data processing (\texttt{FiniteKL.lean})}

The development works with
\[
\mathrm{finiteKL}(p,q)=\sum_aq_a\,\varphi\!\Big(\frac{p_a}{q_a}\Big),\qquad\varphi(x)=x\log x+1-x,
\]
for probability mass functions \(p,q\) on a finite type, where \(\varphi\) is mathlib's \texttt{klFun}. Lean's real division and logarithm are total, so on an atom with \(q_a=0<p_a\) this expression contributes \(0\) rather than \(+\infty\). It is therefore \emph{not} the extended KL divergence on arbitrary pairs. Every theorem below that is about KL carries the support hypothesis \(q_a=0\Rightarrow p_a=0\), under which the identification is proved.

Together these give Theorem~\ref{thm:dpi}. The proof applies Jensen's inequality fibre by fibre: positive fibre mass permits normalized weights, atoms with zero reference mass have zero first-law mass by hypothesis, and empty fibres contribute zero. The commutation hypothesis in the reversal theorem is stated for arbitrary maps \(r,s\); the involution property is used only to interpret \(r_*p\) as the reversed law.

\subsection{Gibbs duality (\texttt{Duality.lean})}

For a nonempty finite type, a cost \(u\), and a real price \(\lambda\), the development defines entropy, mean cost, the partition function \(Z(\lambda)=\sum_ae^{-\lambda u_a}\), and the Gibbs law. The theorems about a general \(q\) share two hypotheses: \(q\) is strictly positive, and \(\log q_a=-\lambda u_a-\log Z\) for some constant \(\log Z\) (Gibbs recognition).

\subsection{Scope}

What is mechanized is finite supported KL data processing and its path form, together with single-row Gibbs duality. The following are proved on paper in Appendix~\ref{app:proofs} but not mechanized: the weighted-row extension, the price regimes and derivatives of Proposition~\ref{prop:regimes}, the reversal-mixture proposition, the packaging bound, and the ladder ranks. The extended-real form of data processing for pairs with infinite KL is standard but also not mechanized. Resource budgets enter the Lean statements only as hypotheses. Nothing in the development derives a budget from packaging or persistence, and nothing verifies the stochastic experiments.
