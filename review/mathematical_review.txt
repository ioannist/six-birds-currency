DECISION AND STRONGEST JUSTIFIED CONCLUSION

The user chose to preserve currency–constraint duality as a conditional result
with explicit resource assumptions. The sound conclusion is:

Given an explicitly supplied maintenance ledger, a feasible inherited budget,
and a finite entropy closure with fixed row weights, Gibbs duality constructs
an optimizer and its nonnegative shadow price in the finite-price regime.
Deterministic observation cannot increase path-reversal KL. The numerical
laboratory illustrates this structure, but packaging/persistence alone does
not imply a resource budget, price positivity, or an optimization principle.

The original finite-KL theorem was correct as stated. Its semantic domain was
strictly positive reference laws and surjective deterministic maps. Both its
coverage and the formal support for conditional duality are now stronger.

The numerical price curve, graph ranks, predictive advantage of the aligned
cost, and stationary packaging-defect reduction survive repair. The old
proxy-price-stability conclusion is unsupported: the old experiment changed
the proxy across seeds and searched designs until the desired result passed.
Corrected fixed-design results contradict that conclusion for the default
example. This subordinate empirical claim must be removed or independently
re-established in a future paper phase; it is not a general consequence of
Gibbs duality. Historical DPI numbers also require replacement because their
finite statistic omitted singular terms. No historical numbers were erased.

1. DEFINITIONS AND THE PACKAGING-TO-RESOURCE BRIDGE

Locations: paper/sections/02_framework.tex, especially the four currency roles,
"The currency–constraint principle", and "Why this is a closure law";
paper/sections/05_discussion.tex, packaging and maintenance inevitability.

A finite carrier, an idempotent packaging map, or a persistent fixed point
supplies no spending law. Counterexample: a one-state identity kernel and
identity packaging map have perfectly persistent packaging with identically
zero cost. More generally persistence can coexist with an externally supplied
unbounded ledger. No implication from persistence to bounded resource use
follows without a resource conservation, viability, or sustainability premise.

A legitimate conditional bridge starts from protocols h, nonnegative ledger
L(h), and a resource law L(h) <= B for admissible maintenance. A macro object
Y with maintenance protocols H_Y is feasible iff some h in H_Y obeys that law.
This is inherited feasibility through an existential maintenance witness, not
a claim that every numerical macro transition is realizable. If the chosen
currency is average cost over a fixed horizon t>0, then L(h)/t <= B/t follows.
Persistence does not supply B or the admissible protocols.

The four current roles need nontriviality conditions if intended to separate
structural currencies from arbitrary statistics. C1 permits K=Y and constant
u; C2 permits the edge cost to ignore u; C3 imposes no explicit connection
between u and U and allows constant U. They are admissible descriptions of
roles, not a sufficient mathematical test of structural importance. A future
strict definition should require essential dependence on the currency, a
proper feasibility boundary or nonconstant potential where relevant, and an
explicit link between the potential and the ledger.

Equality constraints have unrestricted multipliers; the nonnegative price in
the code belongs to an inequality C(q)<=b. Differentiability and uniqueness
are needed for the expression lambda=V'(b). Without differentiability the
correct object is a supporting supergradient of the concave value function.
An active constraint need not have strictly positive multiplier: at the
unconstrained threshold it can be active with lambda=0. A constant cost has
no identifiable price. In thermodynamic notation it is inverse temperature,
with the chosen units, that is the entropy-energy multiplier.

2. FINITE KL, PATH REVERSAL, AND MECHANIZATION

Location: lean/CurrencyMorphism/FiniteKL.lean.

finiteKL(p,q) = sum_a q_a klFun(p_a/q_a),
klFun(x)=x log(x)+1-x. Real division and logarithms in Lean are totalized.
When q_a=0 and p_a>0, the contribution is zero, not infinity; therefore this
real-valued definition is NOT extended KL on arbitrary pairs. This is now
explicit in its docstring. Under support(p) subset support(q), including
common zero atoms, summing the affine terms proves
  finiteKL(p,q) = sum_a p_a log(p_a/q_a),
with zero-numerator contributions interpreted as zero. Under this support
premise it is ordinary finite KL, not merely an unspecified "KL-type" form.

The original theorem remains available with its original signature.
New exports:
  finiteKL_map_le_of_support: arbitrary deterministic f, finite types,
    support(p) subset support(q); no surjectivity requirement.
  finiteKL_map_le_of_pos: positive-reference specialization.
  finiteKL_eq_sum_log_of_support: the ordinary-KL semantic bridge.
  finiteKL_nonneg and finiteKL_self: basic audit laws.
  finiteKL_path_observe_le: actual finite paths Fin n -> alpha, pointwise
    observation, and reversal i -> i.rev; sparse reverse support is allowed.
  reversePath_involutive: reversal really is an involution.

The proof uses Jensen fiber by fiber. Positive fiber mass permits normalized
q weights; zero q terms have zero p by the support premise, and zero-mass
fibers contribute zero. This establishes the claimed deterministic DPI without
adding a theorem-sized hypothesis. The sparse finite-price domain is explicit.
An infinite raw empirical KL is handled by the Python audit; an extended-real
KL theorem on singular pairs has not been newly mechanized.

Fresh build: Lean 4.28.0; locked mathlib revision
8f9d9cff6bd728b17a24e163c9402775d9e6a365. CheckAxioms.lean prints actual
transitive axiom dependencies. The reviewed exports use only propext,
Classical.choice, and Quot.sound. There are no sorry/axiom/admit placeholders.
The trivial bootstrap declarations are scaffolding and establish no paper claim.

3. PATH AUDIT IMPLEMENTATION AND THE OLD DPI EVIDENCE

Locations: src/currencymorphism/audits_pathkl.py;
scripts/exp_dpi_scan.py; frozen docs/experiments/final/dpi_summary.csv.

For the Markov path law p(x)=rho0(x0) product_t P(xt,xt+1),
  log[p(x)/p(reverse x)] = log rho0(x0)-log rho0(xT)
                         + sum_t log[P(xt,xt+1)/P(xt+1,xt)].
Taking expectation gives the implemented boundary-plus-increments formula.
Nonstationary initial laws require the boundary term. Reversibility implies
zero path asymmetry only for a stationary reversible initial law, not for
arbitrary rho0. Exact finite-path enumeration independently checks this
formula, endpoint-support effects (including even/odd periodic paths), and
singular probability mass.

Previously the empirical implementation omitted any window whose reverse
was unobserved but called the remaining finite sum its KL. This is not KL and
need not satisfy DPI. Counterexample with ten length-two windows: nine [0,1]
and one [2,3], observation labels [0,1,1,0]. The old micro finite sum is zero;
the observed law has KL 0.8 log(9)>0. True micro KL is infinite, with singular
mass 1. The frozen runs have positive raw missing-reverse mass (micro mean
0.002052), so the old positive finite margins did not validate KL monotonicity.

Raw empirical audits now return infinity on missing-reverse support. No
windows now raises an error, because there is no empirical probability law.
The analytic audit now computes singular mass, rather than labeling every
singular law as having singular mass 1. With safe kernel S_ij=P_ij when the
reverse edge exists and 0 otherwise, its value is
  1 - sum_{j:rho0(j)>0} (rho0 S^T)_j.
This exactly counts the event that the reversed path has zero probability,
up to ordinary floating-point error in numerical evaluation.

For a finite empirical comparison use the explicitly named reversal mixture:
  p_epsilon=(1-epsilon)p + epsilon R_*p,  0<epsilon<1/2,
  Sigma_epsilon=D(p_epsilon || R_*p_epsilon).
Pointwise observation F commutes with reversal and with this mixture, hence
DPI holds for the SAME empirical sample. This regularized statistic is not
the unsmoothed population KL and is not advertised as an unbiased estimator.
Its reference is positive on the union of observed and reverse support.
Raw missing-reverse mass remains separately visible.

The scan uses fixed sample sizes, epsilon=0.01, and a numerical tolerance of
1e-10. It no longer doubles sample size until a desired sign occurs. The
corrected five-seed minimum mean margin is 0.0037238312341975. This is a
numerical consistency check of a mathematical inequality, not an independent
statistical discovery of DPI. Overlapping windows need no independence for
the same-sample inequality; they would need dependence-aware uncertainty
analysis to estimate population KL.

4. CONDITIONAL MAXIMUM-ENTROPY DUALITY: COMPLETE FINITE DERIVATION

Locations: src/currencymorphism/maxcal_single.py;
lean/CurrencyMorphism/Duality.lean; scripts/exp_budget_sweep.py.

Fix finite nonempty row and destination sets, a finite cost array u_ij, and
fixed weights mu_i>=0 with sum mu_i=1. Define
  H_mu(q)=-sum_ij mu_i q_ij log q_ij,
  C_mu(q)=sum_ij mu_i q_ij u_ij,
with each row of q a probability distribution. These weights do NOT vary with
q; replacing mu by the stationary law of q changes the optimization problem.

For finite lambda, let Z_i=sum_j exp(-lambda u_ij)>0 and
q^lambda_ij=exp(-lambda u_ij)/Z_i. Direct substitution proves
  sum_i mu_i D(p_i || q^lambda_i)
    = -H_mu(p)+lambda C_mu(p)+sum_i mu_i log Z_i.
Putting p=q^lambda gives
  H_mu(p)-lambda C_mu(p) <= H_mu(q^lambda)-lambda C_mu(q^lambda).
For lambda>=0, C_mu(q^lambda)<=b and
lambda(C_mu(q^lambda)-b)=0, every feasible p therefore has
H_mu(p)<=H_mu(q^lambda). The same identity gives the supporting inequality
  H_mu(p) <= H_mu(q^lambda)+lambda(b'-b)
for every p feasible at b'. This is the precise shadow-price meaning.
Rows of positive weight have a unique finite-price optimizer by strict entropy
concavity. Rows of zero weight are unconstrained by this objective and need
not be unique; the code chooses the Gibbs representative for all rows.

Let c0=C_mu(q^0) and cmin=sum_i mu_i min_j u_ij. Differentiation gives
  C_mu'(lambda)=-sum_i mu_i Var_{q^lambda_i}(u_i)<=0.
If some positive-weight row is nonconstant, this derivative is strictly
negative at every finite lambda. C_mu is continuous, decreases from c0 to
cmin, and therefore each cmin<b<c0 has a unique finite positive price.
For b>=c0, lambda=0 and the uniform-row law is optimal (slack for b>c0).
For b<cmin the program is infeasible. At b=cmin the optimizer is uniform on
each row's minimizers and generally requires lambda tending to infinity.
If all positive-weight rows are constant, c0=cmin, the price is unidentifiable.
On the open active interval V'(b)=lambda and
  d lambda/db = -1/[sum_i mu_i Var(u_i)] < 0.
These statements justify the price curve without an empirical monotonicity
assumption. For an equality cost constraint above c0, the unrestricted Gibbs
multiplier can be negative; the nonnegative solver is an inequality solver.

Lean now CONSTRUCTS gibbsPMF from exponentials, proves normalization, strict
positivity, and its log formula. It proves gibbs_KL_identity,
gibbs_penalized_optimal, gibbs_budget_optimal, gibbs_budget_shadow_bound,
and maxent_budget_optimal. The latter supplies the optimizer concretely;
resource feasibility and complementary slackness remain explicit premises.
The formal development is for a finite probability row. The weighted-row
extension, monotone derivatives, inverse derivative, and infinite-price limit
are proved above and checked numerically, not claimed as mechanized.

Solver repairs: minimum endpoint returns the limiting kernel and price +inf;
strictly infeasible budgets are rejected rather than tolerated into a false
solution; slack detection does not erase an active bound within tolerance;
softmax removes row offsets BEFORE multiplying; prices are not artificially
capped at 1e6 (cost units can make a valid price much larger). Numerical roots
remain floating-point approximations with a reported achieved cost.

Interpretation limit: the cost uses the positive part of log(Pij/Pji), with
pseudo-observation smoothing for unobserved macro transitions. This is a
chosen nonnegative ledger, not the full stationary entropy-production rate:
the signed microscopic increment is gauge-sensitive to state potentials,
whereas cycle sums remove those potentials. Full stationary entropy
production uses log[(pi_i Pij)/(pi_j Pji)]. The complete-support macro Gibbs
model can put mass on transitions not realized by the micro substrate.
Consequently the experiment is a synthetic closure on an estimated cost
array, not a construction of every required micro maintenance witness.
The retained price claim is conditional on this modeling choice.

5. CONDITIONAL-LOGIT INFERENCE AND PROXY ABLATION

Locations: src/currencymorphism/mle_logit.py;
scripts/exp_proxy_ablation.py; old proxy meta.json has selected_seed_cost=9
although requested seed_cost=0.

For counts C and row totals N_i,
  ell(lambda)=-lambda sum_ij Cij uij - sum_i N_i log Z_i,
  ell'(lambda)=-sum_ij Cij uij + sum_i N_i E_q[u_i],
  -ell''(lambda)=sum_i N_i Var_q(u_i)>=0.
Identifiability requires a nonconstant cost in some row with N_i>0. Cost
units do not destroy structural identifiability; the old absolute variance
cutoff could incorrectly call a tiny-scale identifiable model unidentifiable.
The corrected information uses centered squares rather than E[u^2]-E[u]^2,
which loses precision under large row offsets.

If all observed destinations minimize their row's cost while some observed
row has nonconstant costs, no finite maximizing price exists. The supremum
is at +infinity; the implementation now reports (inf,inf). A lower-bound
optimum at zero can have positive information, but inverse-information scale
is not a symmetric Wald confidence interval at that boundary. Under model
misspecification it is a curvature scale, not an automatic sampling-error
certificate; a sandwich calculation would be needed for general inference.

The old proxy main loop changed the cost seed until the combined NLL and
variance criterion passed; seed 9 was selected after seeds 0 through 8 did
not meet that conjunction. It also changed the proxy permutation for each
sampling seed. Thus its cross-seed bad-price variance mixed design changes
with sampling variation. The old scalar values reproduce a selected
synthetic example, not unselected evidence of inferential stability.

The repaired driver uses exactly the requested cost seed, a separate fixed
proxy seed across all samples, and reports the criterion even when false.
At default design/permutation seeds 0, the aligned model has mean held-out
NLL 2.10265546097 versus 2.77278983839 for the proxy. Price standard deviations
are respectively 3.18053877347 and 0.554428806331, contradicting the old
stability comparison. No retries conceal that result.

A correct structural prediction claim can be proved: since the synthetic
truth is q_good, any fixed different proxy kernel has expected excess NLL
D(q_good || q_proxy)>=0 (with row weights), strictly positive unless it
matches on all positive-weight rows. This does not imply larger variance
of its fitted price. Prices from different cost coordinates are not a
universal common-scale stability observable. Prediction survives; the
price-stability subclaim must be omitted or established by a separate,
predeclared experiment with a justified comparable metric.

6. RESOLUTION LADDER AND CYCLE COORDINATES

Locations: src/currencymorphism/audits_cycles.py;
scripts/exp_currency_ladder.py; paper/sections/04_results.tex ladder subsection.

For a finite SIMPLE undirected bidirectional-support graph, excluding
self-loops, beta1=m-n+c is the dimension of its cycle space. The map sending
antisymmetric edge forms to their cycle integrals has rank beta1; zero cycle
integrals mean the edge form is a vertex-potential gradient on each connected
component. For bidirectional Markov kernels this is the Kolmogorov cycle
criterion for reversible dynamics within those components. One-way edges
are excluded from this finite-affinity audit, not declared thermodynamically
reversible; they are separately detected by the path KL audit.

Tiny positive bidirectional probabilities were silently discarded by the
old default 1e-15 affinity threshold while the graph used threshold zero.
That mismatch could turn a real cycle drive into zero. The default is now
zero in both places, and invalid cycles or unsupported cycle edges are
rejected. Affinity-vector norms are basis-dependent; comparisons describe
the selected basis, not an intrinsic norm on a basis-free currency space.

For M rings of even length L>=4 and positive bridge weight, with bridges
at 0 and L/2 between adjacent rings, the constructed ladder has EXACT ranks:
  coarse: M vertices, M-1 edges, rank 0;
  middle: 2M vertices, 3M-2 edges, rank M-1;
  fine: ML vertices, ML+2(M-1) edges, rank 2M-1.
For M=6 these are 0,5,11. The code's seeds do not affect the construction;
repeated seed labels are identical deterministic runs, not independent
robustness evidence. Metadata now says so.

This is dimension of AVAILABLE graph cycle coordinates, not the rank of
independently tunable drive parameters. The chosen family has M affinity
parameters, and the fine affinity-family map has rank M=6, not 11. Row
normalization adds only a gradient to log edge ratios; therefore a cycle
integral is linear in the A_m with coefficient equal to signed traversal
count in ring m divided by L. Whole-ring cycles recover each A_m, establishing
rank M. The middle graph has nontrivial cycle space but its affinities vanish
up to roundoff in this symmetric construction. Half-ring translation swaps
the two rows of the macro ladder, preserves the irreducible kernel and its
stationary law, and makes rectangular cycle affinities cancel. Graph capacity
and active drive must not be identified.

No universal monotonic graph-rank law follows from finer resolution. A
four-vertex path is a tree (rank 0); identifying its endpoints gives a triangle
(rank 1), even with reversible dynamics. This counterexample is a regression
test. The correct retained ladder claim is the explicit constructed family's
cycle-space ranks, not a theorem for arbitrary lenses.

7. PACKAGING STABILITY: A PROVABLE REPAIR

Locations: src/currencymorphism/packaging.py;
scripts/exp_idempotence_vs_budget.py.

For row distributions, let B be pushforward followed by stationary-conditional
lifting. Then B^2=B, B is stochastic, and E(mu)=mu P^tau B. Its idempotence
defect is ||mu (P^tau B)^2 - mu P^tau B||_1, between 0 and 2. Small defect of
a given packaged image is not a proof of universal fixed-point convergence.
For stationary prototypes, the maximum over lifted macro basis states equals
the worst defect over lifted macro distributions: convexity gives <=max and
the basis state attaining max gives equality. The reported mean is only a
summary, not that worst-case bound; max is also recorded. For arbitrary
inputs, E(mu) is a lifted distribution, so the exit bound below still applies.

Let pi be stationary with positive mass on every block, and let
  epsilon=max_i sum_{j:f(j)!=f(i)} Pij.
For the conditional prototype nu_a=pi(.|a), stationarity gives
  ||nu_a P - nu_a||_1 <= 2 [stationary outgoing flow from a]/pi(a)
                         <= 2 epsilon.
Indeed inside a, the change is the negative of incoming flow from outside a
divided by pi(a); outside a it is outgoing flow from a divided by pi(a).
Stationarity equates their total masses. Telescope over tau steps using L1
contraction to get ||nu_a P^tau-nu_a||_1<=2 tau epsilon. Since nu_a B=nu_a
and B contracts L1, ||E(nu_a)-nu_a||_1<=2 tau epsilon. Convex combination
extends this to any lifted distribution, including E(mu). Hence
  ||E(E(mu))-E(mu)||_1 <= min(2, 2 tau epsilon)
for every probability mu. This proof uses stationarity, positive block masses,
finite tau, and the stationary prototype; it is not asserted for a uniform
prototype of a nonuniform within-block chain.

The controlled kernel scales inter-block base probabilities by exp(-lambda)
and renormalizes rows. Write r_i for base intrablock mass and s_i=1-r_i.
Then exit probability is
  s_i exp(-lambda) / [r_i+s_i exp(-lambda)].
If every r_i>0 (true in the module-ring laboratory), this tends to zero,
and is bounded by (s_i/r_i) exp(-lambda). Therefore packaging defect tends
to zero with the explicit bound above. This is stronger than the sampled
monotone curve: it is a uniform finite-time vanishing-defect theorem for
stationary prototypes. It does NOT prove that actual defect must decrease
monotonically for every parameter value in every kernel family, since the
prototypes vary with lambda and the upper bound need not be saturated.
If some r_i=0, increasing price may cancel entirely on that row, and no
vanishing-exit conclusion follows.

The experiment's b is from a DIFFERENT auxiliary complete-support macro
Gibbs kernel; it is not the stationary cost of the controlled micro kernel.
The calibration b->lambda is valid, but its achieved cost must not be called
achieved micro maintenance cost. New output includes calibration_cost,
controlled_stationary_cost, controlled_max_exit, and stationary_defect_bound.
For the default sweep calibration minimum is 0.0416667 while minimum actual
stationary controlled cost is 0.000144848. The defect endpoints remain
0.001443300175 and 0.115964475615, and every sampled defect satisfies the
new theoretical bound. The corrected interpretation is enforcement by a
calibrated penalty price, with actual spending separately measured.

9. ADVERSARIAL SELF-REVIEW AND REMAINING COVERAGE LIMITS

The final pass specifically checked:
- positive-reference and common-zero support semantics, empty image fibers,
  actual path reversal and observation commutation;
- no resource law inferred from a fixed point, and no optimizer or price
  existence inferred from an uninterpreted record field;
- Gibbs construction and normalization rather than assumed optimizer input;
- explicit feasibility, inequality-price sign, complementary slackness,
  degenerate costs, zero row weights, and infinite-price boundaries;
- boundary support and periodicity in the analytic path audit;
- sample-law DPI rather than a Markov approximation of observed paths;
- unbiased design reporting rather than retry-until-success evidence;
- cycle-space capacity versus active parameter dimension and basis norms;
- calibration spending versus spending of the actually controlled kernel;
- a stationary-prototype return to the ORIGINAL packaged state space in the
  exit bound, with constants uniform over every input distribution.

Remaining scope is deliberate: Lean mechanizes finite supported KL and
single-row Gibbs conditional duality. It does not mechanize the whole
Markov laboratory, the weighted-array differential calculus, empirical
uncertainty, or the packaging exit estimate. No universal empirical
cross-layer economics law or automatic persistence-to-budget theorem is
claimed by this review. The manuscript awaits the later editing phase and
still contains the original overstatements listed above; this review is the
mathematical handoff for that phase.
